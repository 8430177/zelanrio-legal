# Privacy Policy

**HONG KONG SEVENSHORE TECHNOLOGY LIMITED** (operating as **Zelanrio**, "we", "us", "our")

Last updated: October 17, 2026

## 1. About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect information when we use Login with Amazon and Amazon APIs (including the Amazon Selling Partner API and the Amazon Ads API) to connect our own Amazon seller and advertising accounts to our internal business systems.

Our application is for internal use only. It is not offered to the public or to other Amazon sellers.

## 2. Information We Collect

When we authorize our application through Login with Amazon, we may receive:

- **Account profile information**: name, email address and account identifier of the authorizing Amazon account.
- **Advertising data**: advertising profiles, campaigns, ad groups, keywords, targeting, bids, budgets and performance reports for our own advertising accounts.
- **Seller account data**: orders, inventory, product listings, fees and settlement data for our own seller accounts, as permitted by the API roles we are granted.

We do not request or store Amazon customers' personally identifiable information (PII) unless required for order fulfillment and permitted by Amazon's policies.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use this information only to:

- Manage and optimize our own Amazon advertising campaigns.
- Generate internal reports on sales, inventory and advertising performance.
- Synchronize data between Amazon and our internal business systems.

We do not sell, rent or share this information with third parties for marketing purposes.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- Data is stored on secured servers with access restricted to authorized personnel.
- Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and access credentials such as API secrets and refresh tokens are stored encrypted.
- We follow the Amazon Services API Data Protection Policy and Acceptable Use Policy.

## 5. Data Retention

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes described above, or as required by law. Data we no longer need is deleted.

## 6. Sharing of Information

We do not share information obtained through Amazon APIs with any third party, except:

- When required by law or legal process.
- With service providers that host our infrastructure, under confidentiality obligations.

## 7. Your Rights

The authorizing account holder may revoke our application's access at any time through their Amazon account settings. Upon revocation, we stop accessing the account and delete the associated tokens.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page with an updated date.
